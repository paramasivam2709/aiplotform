Our Story

Built to make AI simple for everyone

Founded in 2024, Stackly started with one belief: powerful AI shouldn't require a PhD. Today our platform serves over 10,000 users across 120+ countries, running millions of AI tasks every month.

We combine cutting-edge models with thoughtful design, so creators, startups and enterprises can focus on ideas — not infrastructure.

  • Mission-first: every feature is designed to save you time and spark better ideas.
  • Privacy by default: your data stays yours, protected by enterprise-grade security.
  • Open ecosystem: connect 200+ tools you already use, from Slack to Notion.
